# PRD implementation map

The user-supplied OTP Autofill PRD is the product reference. Its embedded roadmap and recommendations are requirements context; they do not authorize public deployment, external communications, or account changes. The implemented scope includes the P0 Gmail MVP plus the [twelve edge-case improvements](edge-cases.md), including optional separately authorized inboxes through a local companion. Non-Gmail providers and analytics remain outside this release. The owner subsequently requested configurable Enter after filling; that explicit request supersedes the original no-submission MVP behavior.

## Acceptance boundaries

The initial fixture suite is a controlled regression suite, not evidence that the PRD's proposed 98% extraction or 95% field-detection targets have been met across real websites. Those rates require a representative held-out beta corpus and a documented denominator. The under-ten-second delivery target is subject to API latency, browser throttling, retry backoff, and email delivery, and is not claimed as a measured production guarantee.

Website references and brand words alone do not grant automatic trust. A third-party sender requires explicit confirmation unless explicit website intent, aligned Gmail sender-authentication evidence and the addressed verified inbox support automatic association. Shared or ambiguous intentions require a choice. Complex input patterns and unfamiliar formats abstain. Explicit success signals are heuristic; the extension does not receive authoritative authentication results from arbitrary websites.

See the release checklist for remaining OAuth, store, and controlled-account work.
